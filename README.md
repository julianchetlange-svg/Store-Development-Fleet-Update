# Store Development, Facilities & Fleet — Leadership Update

Interactive update page that ties department work to the three-bucket strategic framework:

1. **Run & Improve** — Keep the Lights On
2. **Proactive Partner** — Efficiency Outside Our House
3. **Revenue & Customer** — Growth & Business Focus

Live page: https://julianchetlange-svg.github.io/Store-Development-Fleet-Update/

## Updating

All content lives in the `CONFIG` and `REPORTS` blocks near the bottom of `index.html`.
Add a new report object to the top of `REPORTS` for each update; older reports stay in the dropdown.
Each item has a `bucket` (1–3), `section`, `status`, `notes`, and optional `links` and `charts`.
Each metrics row has a `bucket`, `metric`, `current` and `detail`.
